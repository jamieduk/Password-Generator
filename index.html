Hash & exposure checker

Enter a password or phrase to calculate common cryptographic hashes and optionally check a password against a known-breach corpus.

Privacy-first breach checkChecks are only meaningful for plaintext passwords. Hashes are not directly searched as proof of a leak. The check uses the Have I Been Pwned Pwned Passwords range service: only the first 5 characters of the SHA-1 hash are sent, not the password or full hash. A match is a warning, not proof that your specific account was compromised.

This tool does not send a password to a website as plaintext. The breach-check feature requires an internet connection and a browser that permits the range-service request. If blocked, use the official Pwned Passwords website manually. MD5 and SHA-1 are included for identification/compatibility only; do not use them to store passwords.

Passwords are generated locally in this page. Avoid entering real passwords on devices or pages you do not trust. Hashes are fingerprints, not encryption: they cannot be “decrypted” in the usual sense.
